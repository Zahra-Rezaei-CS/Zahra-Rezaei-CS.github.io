About me

I hold bachelor's and master's degrees in computer science and am applying for PhD positions at European institutions. My current research focuses on how we evaluate and benchmark hallucinations in multimodal large language models for biomedical applications, with broader interests in the reliability and trustworthiness of language models.

Research

In preparation · targeting Journal of Biomedical Informatics

Hallucination Evaluation and Benchmarking in Biomedical Multimodal Large Language Models: A Methodological Review

Zahra, with collaborator(s)

A systematic review, following PRISMA 2020, of how hallucinations are defined, detected, and measured in biomedical vision-language and EHR-text models. It covers evaluation methodologies, benchmark datasets, clinical relevance, and methodological gaps across 108 included studies.

Multimodal LLMs Hallucination Benchmarking Biomedical AI Systematic review
